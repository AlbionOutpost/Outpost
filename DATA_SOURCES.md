## Market contract and application policy

[Regional schema](https://europe.albion-online-data.com/api/v2/swagger_doc) was consulted for compression, URL/budget and reset semantics. The app requests compressed responses, validates fixed hosts/identities and bounds bytes, timeouts and URLs. Prices with zero sides remain unavailable; daily historical aggregates are not confirmed individual trades. Gold points are observations. The live smoke checks demonstrate sampled access/shape only, not continuous service or source licensing.

Cache TTLs, one-hour staleness display, request budgets, numeric ceilings and artwork concurrency/queue limits are application safeguards, not Albion mechanics or verified upstream capacity. See [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and the actual source implementations under src/lib/albion.

## Original accounting and independent fixtures

All percentages below are supplied test assumptions, not current game fee presets.

- Trade revenue = sale unit price × quantity. Purchase setup = purchase value × supplied purchase fee only for buy-order mode. Cost = purchase value + purchase setup + transport/risk. Sale fees = revenue × (tax + sell setup)/100. Profit = revenue − fees − cost; zero ROI/margin denominators yield null.
- Break-even sale = total cost / (quantity × retained-sale fraction), when positive and within model bounds. Strictly profitable whole-silver sale is floor(exact threshold)+1. Exact decimal ratios handle boundary cases; displayed break-even is approximate.
- Instant fixture: buy100, sell150, quantity10, tax8%, sell setup2.5%, transport50 gives cost1050, fees157.5, profit292.5 and minimum118. Adding buy-order setup2.5% adds25 to cost, giving profit267.5 and minimum121. Repeated listings, partial fills, liquidity and execution probability are excluded.
- Production gross materials = sum(required quantity × supplied unit price). Station cost is supplied per-batch fee × batches. Eligible retained returns reduce economic cost; they do not reduce upfront cash. Profit deducts sale fees and economic cost; cash profit deducts gross inputs. Supplied focus returns/costs do not infer specialization coefficients.
- Crafting fixture: materials2000, station100, revenue3000, tax300 gives cash profit600;20% eligible retained materials gives economic profit1000. Independent refining/enchanting fixtures yield2125/1372. These assert arithmetic, not recipe accuracy or live prices.
- Return-bonus conversion helper uses1−1/(1+bonus/100), with source lead [resource return rate](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Resource_return_rate); current automatic city/event/focus inputs are not implemented.
- Transport computes capacity/trips and supplied per-trip/risk cost; weights/capacity remain manual, not verified mount data. The bounded direct/Black Market scanner preserves source direction and missing values without promising executable profit.

Evidence: [trade model](src/lib/albion/calculations.ts), [production model](src/lib/albion/production.ts), [recipe records](src/lib/albion/recipes.ts), [trade fixtures](tests/calculations.test.ts), [production fixtures](tests/production.test.ts) and [saved-mode browser tests](e2e/production-modes.spec.ts). ## Next milestone mechanics and source scope

Nine T4 equipment recipes use exact ingredient quantities and base focus values from the pinned July snapshot. [Reviewed records](src/lib/albion/reviewed-crafting.json) and [independent constants/accounting](tests/reviewed-recipes.test.ts) cover Battleaxe, Broadsword, Bow, Shield, Scholar Cowl, Mercenary Jacket/Shoes, Bag and Cape. One equipment output per craft is the declared model. The snapshot supplies no matching October patch identifier; the plain-steel source discrepancy and complete refining/other categories remain open.

The bounded focus helper estimates `base focus × 2^(−total focus cost efficiency/10000) × crafts`, following [the historical author's formula and limitations](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/167434-Explaining-Crafting-Tax-and-more/). Base focus is from the July recipe; total efficiency is supplied by the user, including their specialization. No node coefficients, current-patch validity or station rounding are inferred. For base 1,286, efficiency 10,000 and ten crafts, the independent expected estimate is 6,430 focus; at 20,000 and one craft it is 321.5, intentionally fractional. Confirm at the station and enter the actual total before calculating profit/focus. City/bonus rates remain manual. Refining output price identity is always Normal quality, including legacy saved assumptions.
