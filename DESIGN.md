# Design System: Outpost

## Overview

Outpost should feel like a royal city at dusk: warm charcoal stone, ember-orange firelight, painted scenery behind every page, real gear on the table, and every number exactly where a player expects it. It is a **tool first** — players arrive with a job (check a price, find a build, plan a party, cost a craft) and the interface should make that job fast and certain. **Atmosphere is everywhere but never in the way**: each page opens with an illustrated masthead, destination cards carry their own art, and the working panels below stay calm, dense and legible.

The bar is **best-in-class, award-calibre craft**: one coherent visual language across every route, precise typography, deliberate hierarchy, real game art, honest data states and motion that explains rather than decorates. A page is not done until it looks intentional at 1440px and at 390px.

## Colors

Warm charcoal surfaces lit by a single ember accent. Warmth comes from the slightly red-shifted greys, the illustrations and the glow behind gear; depth comes from stepping surfaces (`night` → `panel` → `surface-raised` → `well`). The tokens are defined once, at the top of `src/app/product-system.css` on `.app.outpost` (dark and `.light`): `--bg`, `--panel`, `--elevated`, `--border`, `--text`, `--secondary`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--accent-hover`, `--accent-active`, `--accent-ink`, `--accent-wash`, `--success`, `--error`, `--warning`, the radii and the page widths. Older sheets must not redefine them; framed cards and mastheads re-resolve them only where the palette changes (light-theme cards, mastheads on the painting).

### Primary

- **Ember** (`#ee945a`, `--accent`): primary actions, active nav/tab, selected state, the accent word of a heading, icon-badge rings. Hover brightens to `ember-bright` (`#ffb482`, `--accent-hover`); press deepens to `ember-deep` (`--accent-active`). Text and small glyphs in the accent use `--accent-ink` (`#ffb482`, the readable tint). `ember-wash` (`--accent-wash`) tints selected rows, icon badges and activity chips. Primary buttons use dark ink (`#21150e`) on an ember gradient.

### Neutral

- **Night** (`#101215`, `--bg`): page background. **Night chrome** (`#0c0e11`, `--sidebar`/`--chrome`): header, footer, drawers. **Illustration** (`#0c0d10`, `--illustration-bg`): the canvas the painting settles into, and every shade drawn over it.
- **Backdrop and canvas**: one painting (`backdrop-v1`, the castle at sunset; a 900px phone cut) is fixed behind every page (`.site-backdrop` in the shell), so sections scroll over a single continuous scene and nothing meets at a seam. A light veil sits on it; a scrim that scrolls with the page (`.main::before`) keeps it clear behind the first screen and eases it into `--canvas-end` below (`--backdrop-veil`, `--backdrop-deep`, eased stops), with a fine grain on top (`.main::after`). Both themes use the same painting; only the scrim colour changes. No section paints its own background.
- **Panel** (`#191d22`, `--panel`): legacy sections. **Raised surface** (`--surface-raised` `#1b1e26`): every panel, window and card that sits on the canvas (`.raised`, and `.panel` by default). **Well** (`--well` `#0f1116cc`, `--well-border` `#ffffff12`): inset rows, tables, item wells and controls cut into a raised surface (`.well`). **Elevated** (`#24292f`, `--elevated`): table headers, popovers, menus and hover rows.
- **Roles and sides**: Tank gold `--role-tank`, Healer teal `--role-healer`, DPS violet `--role-dps`, Support blue `--role-support`; killer green `--killer`, victim red `--victim`. Data colour on roster edges and killboards only.
- **Edge** (`#353b43`, `--border`): 1px borders and dividers.
- **Parchment** (`#f1eee7`, `--text`): primary text. **Mist** (`#c5c3be`, `--secondary`): secondary. **Dusk** (`#aaa9a5`, `--muted`): muted captions and timestamps (≥4.5:1 on `panel`).

### Semantic

- **Gain** `#38c88c` (`--success`: profit, rising), **Loss** `#ef6969` (`--error`: negative, falling, destructive), **Caution** `#e6b35b` (`--warning`: stale data, warnings). Focus rings use `--focus`.
- Albion quality and tier colours are game facts; use them only on item badges, never as decoration.

### Named Rules

- **One accent.** Ember is the only brand hue; it never doubles as a semantic colour. Selected controls use `ember-wash` + an ember edge, gains and losses use green and red with a sign or label.
- **Charcoal needs light.** Never ship a page without its masthead art, glow or imagery — flat panels alone were rejected as "lifeless".
- **Profit is never colour-only.** Pair gain/loss colour with a sign or label.
- **Light theme** (`.light`) is Albion's own UI, never white or bright: a dusty brown page `#a68d76`, warm tan windows `#d6b18e` with darker tan rows and inputs `#c4a283`, leather edges `#8a6b50`, ink text `#2a1b10`, muted `#5c4432` and a rust accent `#8f3d14`. Framed cards switch to the dark leather of inventory slots; mastheads and the landing hero keep the night palette because they sit on the painting. Test it separately.

## Typography

Three families, three jobs. All are self-hosted via `next/font/local` (`src/app/fonts.ts`); never load web fonts from a CDN.

- **Barlow Condensed 800** — headings only: the landing hero, masthead titles (uppercase), section headings (mixed case, one accent word in `<em>`), panel and card titles, and headline figures.
- **Inter** — everything else: body, controls, labels, table text.
- **Geist Mono** — every number a player compares: prices, quantities, IP, profit, percentages, timers. Tabular alignment matters more than style.

### Hierarchy

| Role          | Size / weight                                       | Use                                                   |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Display       | `clamp(56px, 6vw, 92px)` / 800 Barlow, uppercase    | Landing hero; 44–76px on system pages (404, error)    |
| Page title    | `clamp(34px, 3.6vw, 52px)` / 800 Barlow, uppercase  | The masthead h1, one per page, names the task         |
| Section title | `clamp(26px, 2.6vw, 34px)` / 800 Barlow, mixed case | `SectionHeading`; the landing's own runs 32–44px      |
| Panel title   | 21px / 800 Barlow (19px phone)                      | `PanelHead`, card and window titles (15–22px)         |
| Body          | 14px / 400, 1.55                                    | Default text; 15–17px for ledes                       |
| Label         | 12px / 600 or 500                                   | Field labels, table headers, timestamps, status lines |

### Named Rules

- **12px floor.** No text below 12px. (The codebase still has ~190 uses of 9–11px; remove them when touching a page.)
- **Five sizes per page, not fifty.** Use the scale; do not invent one-off sizes.
- **Headings name the task.** No slogans or marketing taglines inside the app.

## Layout

- **Content width**: one width per page type, never per tab. `--page-width` (1240px, the landing's measure) for everything a player browses or reads: hubs, lists and libraries (every Builds tab including Favorites, Comps and Group Builds, `/tools`, items, guides, Teams), detail pages (a build, a comp, an item) and account pages. `--workspace-width` (1392px) for the places a player works: every tool under `/tools/*` (calculators, Market Explorer, gold, reference), the Crafting Planner, the build and comp editors, admin. Tabs of one section always share a width. Gutters `--page-gutter`: 32px desktop, 16px phone.
- **Spacing** on the 4/8 scale in the frontmatter. Use the tokens (`--space-*`); do not hand-pick 5/7/14/18px gaps.
- **Inner pages fit one screen** (owner, 7 Nov 2026: "make all our pages one page so you don't need to scroll a lot"). On Builds, Comps, Teams, dashboard, settings and the guides hub the masthead keeps its painting but not the 300px band (no `min-height`, 24px over and 20px under the copy, 16px to the first panel; set in each page's own stylesheet), so at 1440×900 the list, board, editor or first panel starts in the first viewport. Lists put the source switch and the count on the toolbar row, not under a display heading; secondary panels sit beside the primary one rather than under it; pages end on their content, with no closing band of links the masthead or nav already carries (the guest build editor keeps its sign-in band).
- **Breakpoints (target)**: 1280 (wide), 980 (tablet / editor split), 700 (phone layout), 390 (reference phone). The current CSS mostly uses 1100, 780 and 600 plus ~45 ad-hoc values; converge on the targets when touching a page. Design and verify at 1440, 1920, 768, 390, 375 and 320.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces sit on the canvas. Depth comes from one raised surface, not from borders:

- **Raised surface** (`.raised`, `--shadow-raised`): a 1px hairline edge lit at the top (`--raised-edge`, `--raised-edge-top`; a real border, so art inside never covers it), a dark rim and a soft drop; on hover the edge turns ember and the card grows 1.012 (`--shadow-raised-hover`). Working panels (`.panel`) are raised without art.
- **Framed cards** (`.raised.has-scene[data-scene]`): the matching painting covering the whole card under a dark scrim (66% → 86%) and a 4px blur, so it reads as a painted panel rather than glass, so every card keeps its own scene top to bottom, under an **MMO frame**: a bright ember outer line and a fainter inner line following 12px cut corners, corner studs and a diamond gem at the top and bottom centre (`--frame` 9-slice, `--frame-gem`), glowing, brighter on hover. The card paints nothing itself: its painting and scrim are clipped to the same cut-corner shape, so no square corner shows past the frame. Framed grids keep at least 22–26px between cards so gems never touch; content sits at least 16px inside the edge. Used for every card that leads somewhere or holds a live tool: landing windows, tool cards, killboards, activity and plan cards, library builds, Group Build members. Paintings fit the content (battle scenes for group fights and kills, the city for PvE); library builds take one of the five paintings by their id. Landing windows add ember rules under the title bar and above the footer.
- **Wells** (`.well`): what is cut into a surface — tables, rows, item and ability wells, steppers, tabs.
- Popovers, menus, dialogs: `elevated` + stronger shadow; dialogs dim the page.
- **Gear glow**: a warm ember radial behind featured equipment (item wells, build cards, selected item).
- **City colours** (`.city-dot[data-city]` in `product-system.css`): Bridgewatch gold `#e0a53c`, Martlock blue `#4f8fe0`, Thetford purple `#a569e0`, Lymhurst green `#58b764`, Fort Sterling silver `#e8eef5`, Caerleon red `#d9534f`, Brecilien teal `#4fb8c9` — the game's own faction colours, used only as an identity dot beside a city name, never as text colour.
- **Working panels**: every top-level panel on every page (`.panel`, plain `.raised` and the editors' and calculators' own boxes listed in `product-system.css`) is framed like the landing's cards in the dark palette in both themes: the same aged-copper 9-slice frame, drawn on the panel itself as a border image pushed 10px outside, over its own painting under a heavy scrim (86% → 95%) with an offset depth shadow and no coloured halo. No extra layers, no forced positioning, no gems: sticky panels keep sticking and nothing can sit over a neighbouring control. Panels inside panels stay plain. Stacked panels keep 26px between them.
- **Mastheads**: every page opens on the site backdrop (`WorkspaceHeading` with `scene`, or `.masthead[data-scene]` for custom heroes such as build detail) with a shade behind the copy that fades out before the panels — no box edge, no second painting. The emblem is a painted mark for what the page does (`toolEmblem` in `src/lib/search-emblems.ts`) or, on the dashboard and settings, the player's Discord picture. Inside: the emblem well (a tool glyph, an item render or a team monogram via `emblem`), the display title (Barlow 800 uppercase), one line, then `MastheadTabs` (pill tabs for the page's sections and sibling tools), chips (`.landing-chip`) and a back pill (`.pill-link.is-small.masthead-back`); `actions` sits at the right edge. The copy aligns with the page's content edge whatever its max-width. Tools, calculators, timers, the Crafting Planner and the combat feeds pass `compact`: the same painting, shade and emblem in a shorter band (48px emblem, smaller title, the line beside it), so the working view starts about 150px under the header. A page with a painting of its own (a guide's `cover`) shows it across the masthead under the same shade, dissolving into the backdrop below. On phones the shade covers the copy edge to edge, and tab rows that overflow scroll sideways with a fade on the cut edge. No route boxes its masthead; route sheets may tune its padding only. Working panels below never get art of their own.
- **Icon badges**: section and feature icons sit in a 36–44px circle of `ember-wash` with a 1px ember ring.
- Never nest a padded card inside another padded card.

## Shapes

- **Site-wide frames:** aged copper, not illuminated gold. Shared `--frame` runs from `#92715a` through `#684c3c` to `#3e322b`, with a 1.5px outer stroke and a quiet 0.75px inner line. Dark metal studs and gems use `--frame-gem`; the footer rule uses `--frame-rule`. Illustrated cards, top-level working panels and both themes share these tokens. Hover/focus may add a restrained metal highlight, never an orange halo. Keep primary controls and keyboard focus indicators distinct.
- Radii: **7px** controls (`--radius-control`), **12px** raised surfaces and dialogs (`--radius-lg`; framed cards cut 12px corners), **10px** wells (`--radius-md`). Pills (999px) are for tabs, chips, hearts and the "View all" links (`.pill-link`) only.
- Item wells are square with a 6–8px radius and a consistent frame; tier/enchant badges sit in a fixed corner.
- Icons: Lucide at 16/20px for UI; original task emblems (`tool-icon.tsx`) for destinations. Never use item art as a UI icon.

## Components

Shared UI lives in `src/components`. Reuse before you restyle; if two pages need the same thing, it becomes one component.

**Stylesheets.** The root layout loads only what every page needs: tokens, the shell, shared surfaces, controls and motion (16 sheets, `globals.css` through `product-support.css`). Everything else loads with the route that renders it. The build, composition and Team build workspaces import `src/app/build-styles.ts`, one ordered list of their 13 sheets, from the `builds` and `compositions` layouts and the favorites, Team and item pages; guides use their own modules (`guides.module.css`, `article.module.css`) and load none of them; a calculator or feature component imports its own sheet (`journal-workspace.css`, `production-*.css`, `calculators.module.css`, `market-explorer.css`). Route sheets load after every global sheet, and after client-side navigation they can sit after other routes' sheets too, so a route rule must win on its own selector: never rely on a later global rule overriding it (delete the losing declaration instead), and never split one ordered group across routes.

### Buttons

Primary (ember, dark text) for the one main action per view; secondary (panel + edge-strong border) for the rest; tertiary (text + arrow) for navigation links; destructive uses `loss`. Height 40px (36px compact). Hover grows to 1.045 and holds; press compresses to 0.96.

### Forms

One set of controls for the whole site, styled once in product-system.css ("Form controls") with element specificity only, so a layout class can size or place a control but never restyles it:

- **Field well:** every text, number, search, date and time input, select and textarea is the landing's well: `--well` fill, `--well-border` edge, `--well-inset` shadow, `--radius-md`, **42px tall**, 0 12px padding, Inter 500 14px (16px on phones so iOS never zooms). Numbers are typed in Geist Mono with tabular figures; placeholders are muted Inter. Textareas start at 112px and grow vertically. Hover warms the edge toward ember.
- **Label** above the control in Inter 600 12px, muted, mixed case, 6px gap; a visually hidden label (`sr-only`) only where the field's purpose is obvious from its place (a search bar). **Hint** 12px muted under the control. **Error** said once, under the field, 12px in the loss tone. Use `Field` for all three.
- **Focus:** one ring for every control, 2px `--focus` at 2px offset, the edge warming to ember.
- **Disabled:** 55% opacity and the not-allowed cursor; a select's chevron dims with it.
- **Checkbox and radio:** native, 18px, `accent-color: --accent`, its label beside it in 14px secondary text.
- **Compound fields** keep the one well and put their parts inside it: the crafting quantity with its 1×/10×/100× choice, the shopping stepper (− number +), the Team address with its fixed prefix.
- **Choices** of two to eight options are `.segmented`; filter chips that narrow a list (categories, cities) stay chips.
- **File inputs** show the picker as a small well button.

Never add a second look for a control: give a page's field a layout class (width, grid span), not a new border, height or font.

### Motion

Shared rules live in `src/app/motion-system.css` and the surface rules in `product-system.css`; everything runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Tokens: `--ease-out-expo` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for arrival and growth, `--ease-spring` `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.36, 0.64, 1)` for travel.

- Controls: hover scale 1.045, press 0.96 (90ms); equipment 1.06 (180ms).
- Cards (directory entries, link cards, landing windows, tool cards, guide covers, activity and kill cards): grow 1.022 over 300–380ms expo and hold; press 0.985. While hovered an ember light follows the pointer across the card's own surface (`--mx`/`--my` fed by `PointerGlow`, `--glow` registered so it fades). Guide covers grow 1.025 and their painting zooms slowly inside.
- Disclosure: lists open by height (`grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr`, 480ms) inside their own card only, rows arriving 45ms apart; chevrons turn.
- Landing: the backdrop settles from 1.08 over 2.6s; the hero copy rises out of a mask line by line; the three windows deal in after it; every section below rises into place as it enters the viewport (scroll-driven `view()` timelines, neighbours staggered). Only transforms and clip paths move — never opacity — so text never passes through low contrast, and browsers without scroll timelines show everything in place.
- Menus/surfaces open in 180ms; drawers slide 20px in 220ms. Theme change: circular reveal from the switch (720ms).
- Reduced motion removes all transforms and transitions; state changes stay visible.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use real game art from the official renderer (`item-icon.tsx`, `spell-icon.tsx`) to identify actual items and abilities in tables, cards, calculators and loadouts.
- **Do** show where every number came from and how fresh it is; label manual inputs and estimates as such.
- **Do** use Albion vocabulary: **Comps** (not "compositions" in UI), builds, loadout, swaps, IP, spec, focus, RRR, buy order / sell order (never bid/ask), Teams (UI name for guilds/groups), tier notation T4.0 / T6.1.
- **Do** design empty, loading, error, stale and unavailable states for every data view.
- **Do** keep everything keyboard-reachable with visible focus, labelled controls, dismissible dialogs and focus return.
- **Do** compare your rendered page against the matching reference at desktop and phone width before calling it done.

### Don't:

These have all been tried and explicitly rejected — do not bring them back:

- **Don't** use the green/ivory "expedition" palette, or flat navy panels without illustration and glow ("worse and lifeless").
- **Don't** add a site-wide left sidebar, a per-route header, or a filled box behind nav items on hover. (A section rail inside a page — e.g. Market tools, build filters — is fine.)
- **Don't** use cursor tilt, vertical lift on hover, or independent icon hover motion. Grow-and-hold only.
- **Don't** put a chart in the hero, leave a section empty, or use plain boxes / an isometric banner on the homepage.
- **Don't** strip the homepage to an illustration-free equipment collage.
- **Don't** use uniform inventory slot boxes — use the anatomical board.
- **Don't** nest padded cards in padded cards, double-border inputs, or nest scroll regions.
- **Don't** use generic SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, random glow, oversized rounding or decorative item art.
- **Don't** show fabricated activity: votes, likes, view counts, "live" badges, damage/sustain bars, "Verified" labels, invented prices, trends or unsupported IP — even when a reference image shows them. Real equivalents exist: AODP history for trends and sparklines, traded volume, observation ages, library update dates.
- **Don't** put migration history, competitor names (including "Grind" taglines), slogans inside tools or engineering explanations in the public UI.
- **Don't** present generated illustrations as gameplay, use authentic game scenes without permission, or copy competitor code, art or copy.
