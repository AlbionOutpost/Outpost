What we work on

Three areas we keep coming back to. Outpost covers the first two; the third is still mostly on the workbench.

  • Player tools

    Calculators, planners and live data for games people already play. Quick to use and clear about where every number comes from.

  • Web products

    Sites and web apps that stay fast on any phone, work in light and dark, and collect only what they need to.

  • Games and experiments

    Small games and prototypes. Some will ship, some will stay on the workbench. We will share the ones worth your evening.

A small studio, on purpose

Hollow Lantern is an independent studio based in Germany. We build what we want to use as players: tools that answer a question in seconds, and games that are worth an evening.

Staying small means every decision is ours to make, and every detail gets a second look. We would rather ship one thing that works well than five that almost do.
