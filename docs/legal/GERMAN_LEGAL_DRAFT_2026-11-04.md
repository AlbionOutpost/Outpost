# German legal information: evidence and activation gaps

Status: draft implementation for `/legal`, reviewed against source and official publications on 4 November 2026. This is an engineering/content record, not a legal opinion or compliance certification. Operator identity, business organization and several operational decisions remain unknown. Placeholder publication does not satisfy missing statutory particulars or authorize commercial operation. No payment, advertising or consent runtime is enabled by these legal pages.

## Deliverable and scope

The German reading page keeps `/legal` and the existing `#operator`, `#privacy`, `#data-sources`, `#scene-artwork` and `#media` links. It adds navigable cookie, provider, retention, rights, usage, payment and withdrawal/cancellation sections. A visible draft notice and unresolved operator fields remain even if individual existing environment fields are populated. Metadata is `noindex, follow` while the page is incomplete; no new legal slugs are introduced. This is a discoverability choice, not a substitute for supplying legally required notices. The SEO owner was notified.

The page uses the established display/body fonts and copper/charcoal theme, a bounded reading column, desktop contents navigation, a wrapping phone index and a focusable horizontal cookie table. It distinguishes the official item renderer, self-hosted fonts, actual current decorative AI-assisted scenes and community/game content. The former claims that illustrations were merely archived and that no advertising-cookie functionality existed were inaccurate and are removed.

## Explicit unresolved activation requirements

1. Identify and publish the real operator, service/contact address, email and applicable registration/tax/representation details. Confirm legal jurisdiction and responsible authority; do not invent a VAT or register number.
2. Complete purpose-specific legal bases/interests, recipient/entity/processor agreements, transfer assessment, security/log inventory, documented retention/backups and functioning data-subject request contact. Finalize Art. 13/14 notice against actual deployment, not just local source.
3. Keep AdSense script and ad requests off until a real consent platform, revocation behavior, publisher/provider requirements and Supporter suppression are reviewed and verified. No custom uncertified banner or invented TCF string is proposed.
4. Keep payments unconfigured until real prices/taxes, terms, term/renewal/cancellation rules, contract-confirmation channels and actual consumer rights mechanisms are supplied. Existing checkout does not capture a reviewed withdrawal/terms acceptance evidence chain.
5. Implement and review separate applicable §312k cancellation and §356a withdrawal functions and acknowledgments. Do not conflate account deletion, portal cancellation and statutory withdrawal.
6. Confirm user-content licensing/moderation/redress and minor-user contract handling. Preserve mandatory liability/consumer rights rather than using a broad exclusion.
7. Review concrete AI-assisted asset inventory, source permissions and any applicable content/metadata-specific Article 50 duties. A public general disclosure does not create licenses or verify every asset.
