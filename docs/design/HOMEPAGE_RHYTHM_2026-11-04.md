## Problem and design direction

The previous homepage placed three equally weighted planning windows above three equally weighted live tool widgets. Inside a 376px desktop tool card, multiple frame/panel paddings, a 52px item image and a 96px stepper left about 112px for the recipe name and description. Repeated small panels and simultaneous prices competed with the product hierarchy. The old 1100px outer-column breakpoint also disagreed with 769–1150px internal window rules, so narrower cards could become more compressed than wider ones.

The revised layout keeps Outpost's existing Barlow Condensed/Inter/Geist Mono typography, charcoal/copper surfaces and supplied illustrations. Two related planning examples share a larger row, Team capabilities form a separate companion strip, and the tools area shows one working preview with three persistent selectors. More space accompanies fewer simultaneous surfaces; this is not a claim that every section or the whole page became shorter.

Desktop section separation is 112px, with 32px planning gaps. Planning begins 64px after the hero rather than overlapping its edge. Phone section spacing is 88px, with 48px before planning. The tool choices use 16px separation and 20px internal padding; the main selector/preview split has a responsive 32–72px desktop gap, reduced to 28px at narrower widths. At 780px and below, choices become a horizontal row above the preview. Planning pairs stack at the phone breakpoint. Container rules, rather than the previous contradictory viewport rules, wrap narrow example titles and roster names.

## Planning content and destinations

The two examples remain actual public library content: Battleaxe Mist Build and the Hellgate 5v5 composition with its recorded equipment, selected abilities and member roles. Example-title links still open their public library detail routes with the selected region. The distinct primary actions now open `/builds/new` and `/compositions/new`, also preserving region. Existing account-like controls retain their client/server behavior and private response boundaries.

The Team companion describes capabilities, not a fabricated guild dashboard or invented private activity. Its copy explains that a Supporter runs a Team and members join free; the five capability names retain their existing explanatory text in titles. The action opens `/groups` with the region. No actual private guild data, OAuth session, event, loot split or saved scenario is created by the local preview checks.

The paired bodies are bounded around 304px, with compact 44px title regions and 82px action regions. The desktop public examples measure 572px wide and 470px high in the planning evidence. At 320px, an early stacked-armor arrangement made the comp body about 427px; keeping armor inline reduced it to about 308px while preserving the recorded members and equipment. Narrow weapon names wrap instead of being silently truncated.

## One active tool, preserved state

`ToolShowcase` keeps all three real `PreviewShell` widgets mounted. Only the selected panel is visible and interactive. Inactive panels have `visibility: hidden`, `inert`, `aria-hidden`, disabled pointer interaction and a negative tab index. They remain in one shared grid cell so the preview frame reserves the greatest natural panel height and does not jump during tool switching. This deliberately preserves market range/selection, crafting batch quantity, planner ownership and the planner's internal tab. It does not persist them across a page reload.

The selectors have a named tablist, connected tab/panel IDs, selected state and one roving tab stop. Desktop vertical choices support directional navigation; compact horizontal choices preserve Up/Down for page scrolling. Home/End select the ends. Activation follows focus because the real panels are already mounted. The active panel is keyboard reachable, and inactive controls leave the accessibility tree and tab sequence. This follows the relevant [WAI-ARIA APG tabs guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/). Native inert behavior and its focus/interaction implications are documented by [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/inert).

All existing quote requests and regional invalidation remain. Hidden widgets remain mounted and can fetch as before; this revision does not claim fewer requests or a measured performance improvement. Local development Strict Mode produced two effect lifetimes for craft/planner requests in the captured harness. Those doubled development requests are recorded, not presented as a production budget regression or a production optimization measurement. No price API, economic formula, recipe, fee, return rate, authentication or provider contract changed here.

The Market and crafting material tables retain their named, focusable native horizontal scroll regions. All financial columns remain rendered; small screens scroll to unit price, Total and Spread rather than deleting them. Existing higher-specificity restoration rules remain necessary against older phone styles. Wider presentation does not turn missing observations into zero prices or invent a profitable city.

## Review corrections

An initial axe label/content mismatch was corrected structurally: explanatory selector copy sits outside the named tab button and is referenced as its description. The real visible tab title remains part of the accessible name. The assertion was not disabled.

Independent pointer review also found that global button hover scaling changed the containing block for the tab's stretched pseudo-element. The apparent full-tile target then collapsed to the inner label, making emblem, helper and padded-edge clicks unreliable. A scoped rule keeps the inner tab button untransformed on hover/press; the outer option owns visual feedback. This preserves the whole option target without removing the surrounding motion treatment. The existing homepage regression now performs repeated actual edge clicks after hover settling, in addition to keyboard and input-persistence checks.
