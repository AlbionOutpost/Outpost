- Recovered loadout price checks require recent selected-city observations for every equipped item before showing a total. One unit per equipped slot is priced; consumable stack quantities are not inferred. Missing/stale prices never count as zero.
- Guide discovery now uses the original cover imagery and a featured story. Thirty-two owner-supplied cover/instructional images are copied from allowlisted local raster files, verified and hashed. Two source slugs contain trailing spaces, and one conflicts after trimming. The import retains original source slugs, creates a unique local route for the collision and records each normalization in `public/library/grind-manifest.json`. No record is silently lost.

## Verification and open boundaries

Fishing accounting is original arithmetic with explicitly supplied silver/unit, fish count, chops/fish, purchase and sale fee percentages, and total processing cost. Upfront cash buys every fish before processing; opportunity advantage compares chopped net proceeds minus processing with whole-fish net proceeds, so caught fish do not silently become free. Sale-side selection distinguishes the highest observed buy order from a planned lowest sell listing. Neither implies available stock or a completed trade. Unknown observations stay unknown, while an intentional manual zero remains valid and survives refresh. Fees are user assumptions, not current-game defaults. Totals above one trillion silver and invalid quantities are rejected;100% sale fee has no finite break-even. Fame uses whole-fish ceiling for an entered target and actual editable fame/fish; level thresholds, Learning Points and automatic buff multipliers are excluded.

Independent fish fixture:10 fish at100 silver plus2.5% purchase fees and50 processing cost require1075 silver. Thirty chops at50 less10% sales fees return1350, for275 cash profit. Raw sale at120 less10% returns1080, for55 cash profit; chopping improves the alternative by220. Break-even is1075/27 silver/chop. An entered10000-fame target at210 fame/fish needs48 fish;1000 silver/fish plus2.5% costs49200. Tests use a numeric tolerance for binary floating point; no intermediate game rounding is invented.

Independent recipe fixture: two captured Mutton Stew batches require 24 potatoes at100, 24 bread at200 and48 meat at300. Materials21600 plus station100 require21700 upfront;20 meals at2000 produce40000 gross,4000 supplied tax and14300 cash/economic profit. These supplied prices/tax are arithmetic fixtures, not live game observations. Major Healing retains five outputs and72 foxglove/18 eggs/18 alcohol per captured batch.
