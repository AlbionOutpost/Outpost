## October27 product implementation

The [processed recipe manifest](../data/production-recipes-source.json) records the October12 client-data revision and SHA256. Only minimal factual fields are transcribed; the archive has no declared redistribution license and no blanket legal approval is asserted. Plain Steel Bar is **2 Iron Ore +1 Bronze Bar**, base focus54, separately from Pristine Steel Bar. All nine older T4 ingredient/base-focus records agree with that archive. Official resource-name observations are independently identified and hashed. This is not current-patch certification.

[Production profiles](../../src/lib/albion/production-profile.ts) distinguish production city from purchasing/selling markets. Five Royal cities use the sourced historical bonus formula; manually confirmed daily events are separate. Refining efficiency uses five bounded T4–T8 nodes. Crafting total efficiency stays manual. Applying a profile changes return assumptions and eligible inputs, preserves prices/fees, and persists the profile. Estimated focus remains fractional and is **not** silently substituted for actual station-rounded consumption.
